# Hi, I'm Chanu 👋

I'm a graphics and gameplay programmer who just wrapped up a Master's in Interactive Technology (Software Development) at SMU Guildhall. I like games that look as good as they play, and I spend most of my time building the systems that make that possible: renderers, engines, and the gameplay code that sits on top of them.

## What I do
Real-time rendering — Direct3D 11/12, DXR, HLSL, path tracing, and compute-driven lighting
Engine architecture — multi-threaded job systems, bindless rendering, memory/descriptor management, and networking
Gameplay programming — player movement, AI, weapons, and camera systems in Unreal Engine 5

My go-to custom C++ engine runs on Direct3D 11 and 12, with a bindless renderer, DXR ray tracing pipelines, and a multi-threaded job system under the hood.
